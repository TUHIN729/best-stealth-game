A top-down stealth game: you are a cat burglar slipping through a moonlit museum to steal the Moonstone Idol and reach the exit unseen. Guards patrol with sweeping vision cones, lamp light exposes you while cold shadow hides you, and a suspicion meter gives you a chance to break away and recover — across three hand-designed galleries of rising difficulty, with synthesised footsteps, alert stings and pickup chimes.

## How to play

Serve `game/` with any static server (for example `python3 -m http.server` from inside `game/`) and open `index.html` in a desktop browser. Move with WASD or the arrow keys, or click the floor to walk there; hold Shift to sneak quietly, hold Ctrl to run fast and loud. Steal the relic, then reach the green exit. P pauses, R retries a gallery, M mutes.

Deterministic screenshot scenes: open with `?shot=start`, `?shot=play`, `?shot=alert` or `?shot=win` (plus `&seed=N` to vary the patrol phase).
